  • Farewell to the World I Know

    Mar 2023 - May 2024

    For my senior thesis, I wrote an original play about my grandmother's childhood as a German Jew during the rise of Nazism. In spring 2024, I directed and produced a heavily-staged reading of the play as part of the Brandeis Department of Theater Arts' Senior Studio Festival. I was greatly honored to receive the Harold and Mimi Steinberg Prize for Best Original Play for this deeply personal play.
